Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Short version: Cascade collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no ads. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Cascade does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We have no servers that receive data about you, no user accounts, and no third-party analytics or advertising.

Your settings stay on your device

Your preferences — volume, timer choices, mute, and whether audio was playing — are saved locally on your own device (in your browser's local storage on the web, or in the app's local settings on native builds). This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Network use

The web app downloads only its own files — the application code and the waterfall audio recording — from cascade.stephens.page. It makes no requests to third-party services and sends no usage or telemetry data. The native Windows, Android, and Apple builds play their bundled audio and integrate with your operating system's media controls; they make no data-collection network calls.

Cookies and tracking

Cascade uses no cookies and no tracking technologies. The web app may cache its own files on your device (via a service worker) so it can run offline; this cache is never used to identify or track you.

Children

Cascade is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Because Cascade collects no data, changes are expected to be rare.
